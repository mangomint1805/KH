# hand
grammar|"Do you know where the meeting ____?"|is|is it|does it|it is be
grammar|"Can you tell me when ____?"|the store opens|does the store open|opens the store|the store does open
grammar|"You're coming to the party, ____?"|aren't you|don't you|are you|won't you
grammar|"She finished the contract, ____?"|didn't she|doesn't she|did she|wasn't she
grammar|"The printer isn't working, ____?"|is it|isn't it|does it|doesn't it
grammar|"They have met before, ____?"|haven't they|don't they|didn't they|aren't they
tip|Câu hỏi lựa chọn "A or B?": đáp án đúng thường…|Chọn A, chọn B, cả hai hoặc không cái nào|Bắt đầu bằng Yes|Bắt đầu bằng No|Là một con số
tip|"Should we take a taxi or the bus?" Đáp án hợp lý nhất?|Whichever is faster.|Yes, we should.|At the bus stop.|I took it yesterday.
tip|Câu trần thuật: "The photocopier broke down again." Đáp án hợp lý?|I'll call the repair team.|Yes, it's a copy.|At the front desk.|Two copies, please.
tip|Câu hỏi gián tiếp "Do you know who…?" thì nghe kỹ từ nào?|Từ để hỏi bên trong (who)|Chữ "Do"|Chữ "know"|Chữ cuối câu
colloc|Can you ____ the invoice today?|approve|agree|accept to|apply
colloc|We need to meet the ____.|deadline|time line|end|finish
colloc|Let's ____ the meeting until Friday.|postpone|delay to|cancel to|post
colloc|The train is supposed ____ at 9.|to arrive|arriving|arrive|arrived
