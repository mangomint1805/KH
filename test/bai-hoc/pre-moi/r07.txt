# hand
grammar|This job is ____ than my old one.|easier|easy|more easy|easiest
grammar|She is the ____ candidate in the group.|most experienced|more experienced|experiencedest|experienced
grammar|Our new office is ____ bigger than the old one.|much|very|most|many
grammar|The interview was ____ difficult as the test.|as|so|than|more
grammar|He works ____ than his coworkers.|harder|more hard|hardest|hardly
grammar|This is the ____ training program in the city.|best|better|good|most good
grammar|This year's salary is ____ than last year's.|higher|high|highest|more high
grammar|Of all candidates, Tom is the ____.|youngest|younger|young|most young
form|So sánh hơn của "bad" là?|worse|badder|more bad|worst
form|So sánh nhất của "far" là?|farthest / furthest|farer|most far|farrest
tip|Thấy "than" sau chỗ trống → chọn…|So sánh hơn|So sánh nhất|So sánh bằng|Danh từ
tip|Thấy "the ... of / in" → thường chọn…|So sánh nhất|So sánh hơn|So sánh bằng|Động từ
colloc|We are looking ____ an office assistant.|for|at|after|up
colloc|Please send your resume for further ____.|information|informations|inform|informative
