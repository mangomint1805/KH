# hand
grammar|He usually ____ by train.|travels|travel|is travel|traveling
grammar|Yesterday, we ____ from Hanoi at 8 a.m.|departed|depart|have departed|will depart
grammar|Look! The shuttle bus ____ now.|is arriving|arrives|arrived|has arrived
grammar|I ____ in this company since 2020.|have worked|work|worked|am working
grammar|She ____ already ____ in.|has / checked|is / checking|did / check|was / check
grammar|When I arrived, the train ____ already ____.|had / left|has / left|was / leaving|will / leave
grammar|By next month, we ____ the project.|will have finished|have finished|finished|had finished
grammar|We ____ the client tomorrow.|will meet|met|have met|meeting
grammar|At 9 p.m. last night, I ____ my email.|was checking|am checking|check|have checked
tip|Dấu hiệu "since, for, already, yet" → thì nào?|Hiện tại hoàn thành|Quá khứ đơn|Tương lai đơn|Hiện tại tiếp diễn
tip|Dấu hiệu "yesterday, last week, ago" → thì nào?|Quá khứ đơn|Hiện tại hoàn thành|Tương lai đơn|Hiện tại đơn
tip|"isn't" là viết tắt của…|is not|is no|it not|in not
colloc|We arrived ____ Tokyo at night.|in|at|on|to
colloc|Please get ____ the bus at the next stop.|off|out|down|away
colloc|Please book the room ____ advance.|in|on|at|for
