grammar|The machine ____ yesterday.|was fixed|fixed|has fix|is fixing
grammar|The report must ____ by Friday.|be submitted|submit|submitted|be submitting

grammar|The new procedure ____ next week.|will be introduced|will introduce|introduced|is introducing
