grammar|"____ you going to the concert?" – Yes, I am.|Aren't|Don't|Didn't|Won't
grammar|"The fax machine works, ____?"|doesn't it|isn't it|does it|won't it
grammar|"You've met Ms. Lee, ____?"|haven't you|didn't you|don't you|aren't you
grammar|"Do you know ____?"|who won the contest|who did win the contest|won who the contest|who the contest win
tip|Câu hỏi phủ định "Didn't you go on vacation?" – Nếu CÓ đi, trả lời…|Yes, I went to Bali.|No, I went to Bali.|No, I did.|Yes, I didn't.
tip|Câu hỏi đuôi: trả lời giống như câu hỏi…|Khẳng định bình thường|Phủ định ngược nghĩa|Lựa chọn|Câu Who
tip|"Should I call or email the client?" Đáp án hợp lý?|Calling is faster.|Yes, you should.|Two clients.|At the office.
tip|Câu hỏi "Or" thường KHÔNG trả lời bằng…|Yes / No|Chọn 1 trong 2|"Either is fine"|"Neither"
