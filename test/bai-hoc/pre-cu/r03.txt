# hand
grammar|Our staff ____ complaints every day.|handle|handles|is handle|handling
grammar|The company ____ the new software last year.|released|releases|has released|will release
grammar|Profits have increased ____ since 2023.|drastically|drastic|drastical|drasticness
grammar|She ____ the presentation now.|is outlining|outlines|outlined|has outline
grammar|We ____ the donation already.|have received|receive|are receiving|will receive
grammar|They ____ a new policy next month.|will adopt|adopted|have adopted|adopting
grammar|When I called, he ____ a meeting.|was having|has|is having|had had
grammar|Before she joined us, she ____ in Seoul.|had worked|has worked|works|is working
tip|Dấu hiệu "now, at the moment" → thì…|Hiện tại tiếp diễn|Quá khứ đơn|Hiện tại hoàn thành|Tương lai đơn
tip|Dấu hiệu "every day, usually" → thì…|Hiện tại đơn|Quá khứ đơn|Tương lai đơn|Quá khứ tiếp diễn
colloc|We need to sort ____ this problem.|out|up|on|off
